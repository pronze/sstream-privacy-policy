Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the SStream app for mobile devices, together with any related services operated by pronze (collectively, the "Application"). pronze is hereby referred to as the "Service Provider".


Information Collection and Use

When the Application experiences a crash, performance degradation, or when you manually submit a bug report, the following information is securely processed:

  • Application State Data: Interaction traces (logs of clicks, swipes, and scrolls) and internal app logs to help replicate the bug.
  • Visual Error Diagnostics: Visual view-hierarchy details captured at the exact moment a crash occurs to help debug interface failures.
  • Device Technical Specifications: Information regarding your mobile operating system, device model, hardware specifications, and system crash tombstones.

Cookies and tracking technologies

The Application or its third-party SDKs may use cookies, SDKs, pixels, and similar technologies to support functionality, analytics, or service delivery. Where required by applicable law, the Service Provider will obtain consent before using non-essential tracking technologies.

Your Rights

Because the Application only collects anonymized diagnostic and crash data without tracking or storing personal user identifiers, we do not maintain identifiable user profiles. Consequently, we cannot link collected telemetry to individual users to look up or isolate records for deletion. You can stop all data collection at any time by uninstalling the Application or by disabling error reporting settings if available.


Data Retention Policy

The Service Provider retains data based on its necessity for the stated purposes:

  • Crash and Diagnostic Logs: Retained in an anonymized, aggregated format for up to 24 months by third-party diagnostic services (such as Sentry) for app stability analysis, after which they are automatically purged. These logs do not contain persistent user identifiers.

Opt-Out Rights

You can stop further collection of information from your mobile device by disabling Share Diagnostic Data in application settings.


Third Party Access

Only aggregated, anonymized data is periodically transmitted to external services to aid the Service Provider in improving the Application and their service.

Where applicable law requires safeguards for international transfers, the Service Provider will use appropriate mechanisms.

  • Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the European Commission
  • Adequacy decisions or other legally recognized transfer mechanisms
  • Your consent, where required and legally permitted

Data protection laws in other countries may differ from those in your jurisdiction. Where required by law, the Service Provider will apply appropriate safeguards and obtain any consent required for the transfer.
